Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone, in a private local database.
  • We do not create accounts, run servers, or collect your data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • You can erase everything at any time from inside the app.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Serenity (the “App”), a mental wellness and mood tracking application for Android provided by Honestopedia Tech Sol (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information. We built Serenity to be private by design, and this document describes what that means in practice.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information stored in the App

Serenity stores the following information that you choose to enter. All of it is saved locally on your device:

CategoryDetails
ProfileFirst name (or nickname), age, your chosen focus or goal, and your preferred daily check-in time.
Check-insMood rating, energy level, hours of sleep, selected tags (such as Work or Family), optional written notes, and the date and time of each entry.
PreferencesApp settings such as light or dark mode.

Health-related entries are sensitive. That is why they never leave your device through the App.

3. What we do not collect

Serenity does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, profile, check-ins, notes, or any other content you enter.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.
  • Payment information (the App is free and has no purchases).

The App does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not request internet access to function.

4. How your information is used

Information you enter is used only inside the App on your device, to show your history, calculate streaks and insights (such as weekly mood charts and average sleep), and personalise your greeting. Insights are calculated locally and never sent anywhere.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information. Because we do not receive your data, we have nothing to disclose to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics software.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored in a private database inside the App's protected storage area on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. We recommend that you also protect your phone with a screen lock, fingerprint, or face unlock.

Device backups

Depending on your phone's settings, Android or your device manufacturer may include app data in its own backups (for example, Google backup) or transfer it when you move to a new phone. That process is controlled by your device and your account settings, not by the App. You can review or turn off device backups in your phone's system settings.

7. Retention and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You are in full control:

  • Delete a single entry: in the Journal tab, swipe the entry to the left.
  • Erase everything: go to Profile and choose “Erase all my data”. This permanently deletes your profile and all check-ins.
  • Uninstall the App: removing Serenity from your phone deletes its locally stored data.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it once it has been deleted.

8. Your rights

Since all information remains under your control on your own device, you can access, correct, or delete it directly in the App at any time. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights regarding personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is generally nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our side. If you have any questions about your rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's privacy

Serenity is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing in your country, please use the App only with a parent or guardian's involvement.

10. Not medical advice

Serenity is a self-reflection and wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice. It is not a substitute for care from a qualified health professional. If you are struggling or in crisis, please contact a licensed professional or your local emergency services right away.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. If we make a meaningful change, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, tell you inside the App. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
